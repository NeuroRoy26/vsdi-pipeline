PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This repository contains a suite of MATLAB scripts designed for the processing, 
analysis, and visualization of functional imaging data (specifically Delta F/F 
movies from Voltage-Sensitive Dye recordings). The pipeline provides tools to 
assess the spatial spread, temporal dynamics, statistical thresholding of 
cortical activation, and rigorous artifact diagnostics.

REFERENCE & INSPIRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The methodology and analysis goals in this project were initially inspired by 
and modeled after the following paper:

Morales-Botello, M. L., Aguilar, J., & Foffani, G. (2012). Imaging the 
Spatio-Temporal Dynamics of Supragranular Activity in the Rat Somatosensory 
Cortex in Response to Stimulation of the Paws. PLoS ONE, 7(7), 
e40174. 

Specifically, this pipeline attempted to replicate the paper's approach to 
investigating the spatio-temporal dynamics of the somatosensory cortex. This includes 
efforts to isolate the neuronal signal by subtracting unstimulated sweeps to 
remove bleaching and physiological noise. While the exact results or 
clean thresholding described in the paper proved difficult to fully replicate 
with the current dataset, the scripts retain the fundamental analytical 
approaches, such as calculating active pixels, comparing latencies, and 
assessing spatial spread.

SCRIPT DESCRIPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. determine_pixel_area.m
   - Purpose: Calibration utility to map image pixels to real-world physical 
     dimensions.
   - Details: Attempts to read HDF5 hardware metadata first. If unavailable, it 
     auto-fills based on known lab crop sizes or provides an interactive GUI to 
     manually measure objects of known size. Saves data to pixel_calibration.mat.

2. single_frame_grabber.m
   - Purpose: Command-line utility for exploring the raw structural HDF5 data.
   - Details: Reads the /structural dataset and provides a text-based menu to 
     scrub through frames, jump to specific timepoints, and export specific 
     frames as .png images for reference.

3. plot_roi_analysis.m
   - Purpose: Primary feature-extraction script for visualizing neural dynamics.
   - Details: Extracts time-series traces from two specific 10x10 pixel Regions 
     of Interest (ROIs). Generates a global energy plot, creates a 4x4 montage 
     of frames around the stimulus onset, and generates a space-time kymograph 
     to visualize wave propagation across a spatial slice.

4. activity.m
   - Purpose: Broad spatial overview of signal behavior across the imaging field.
   - Details: Divides the field of view into a 5x5 grid, averages the temporal 
     Delta F/F trace within each square, and plots all 25 traces in a unified 
     figure with locked Y-axes for direct amplitude comparison across regions.

5. analyze_threshold_sensitivity.m
   - Purpose: Sweeps through multiple threshold multipliers (3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x) 
     to optimize activation masking.
   - Details: Calculates per-pixel baseline statistics and loops through the 
     multipliers to generate threshold maps and activation masks for all frames. 
     Saves individual masks to separate HDF5 files, creates comparative figures, 
     and recommends the best multiplier based on the maximum separation between 
     baseline and post-baseline activity.

6. define_activated_pixels.m
   - Purpose: Statistical thresholding to separate stimulus-evoked signal from 
     baseline noise.
   - Details: Calculates the mean and standard deviation of a defined pre-stimulus 
     baseline period for every individual pixel. Creates a binary activation mask 
     using a "Mean + 5*StdDev" threshold and exports the results to .mat and .h5 
     files. (Note: analyze_threshold_sensitivity.m largely supersedes this).

7. visualize_activation_masks.m
   - Purpose: Analytical dashboard to evaluate the strictness of different 
     threshold multipliers.
   - Details: Loads and compares masks generated by 3x and 4x standard deviation 
     multipliers. Generates extensive figures including frame-by-frame activation 
     percentages, bar charts of pre- vs. post-stimulus activity, and overlays 
     of the binary masks onto raw Delta F/F colormaps.

8. inspection_neuralresponse_or_globalartifact.m
   - Purpose: Interactive quality-control (QC) tool for manual data inspection.
   - Details: Generates a GUI with a slider to scrub through the movie frame-by-
     frame. Displays the raw signal, the binary mask, and an overlay side-by-side 
     to help the user visually differentiate between genuine localized neural 
     responses and global imaging artifacts (e.g., photobleaching).

9. diagnostics_activation_analysis.m
   - Purpose: Comprehensive automated diagnostics for VSD activation masks.
   - Details: Checks for photobleaching using a 15-frame running mean and 
     automatically flags suspicious frames based on robust thresholds (e.g., 
     global mean z-score jumps, massive spikes in percent active pixels, or 
     frame-to-frame motion proxies). Creates per-frame diagnostic image arrays 
     for flagged frames.

SUGGESTED WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Run determine_pixel_area.m to establish spatial calibration.
2. Run single_frame_grabber.m to visually inspect structural anatomy.
3. Run plot_roi_analysis.m and activity.m to understand raw signal dynamics.
4. Run analyze_threshold_sensitivity.m to sweep through standard deviation 
   multipliers (3x to 7x) and identify the mathematically optimal threshold. 
   This will automatically generate your mask HDF5 files.
5. Use visualize_activation_masks.m to visually compare the top two or three 
   masks generated in step 4.
6. Run diagnostics_activation_analysis.m to automatically flag frames suffering 
   from drift, motion, or massive global artifacts.
7. Use inspection_neuralresponse_or_globalartifact.m to manually review the 
   specific suspicious frames flagged in step 6.
